Rules of the ledger

MSRP is the floor you buy at, and it never lasts. The 2021 Celebrations ETB is 7.1× list today, earned from retail over five years. Paying 3.7× presale turns that into a double.

Six weeks after launch is the entry for anything secondary. Pitch Black chase singles lost 45% in six weeks; OP-16 boxes fell from $400 to $188 in a month. Ascended Heroes is the only 2026 set that broke the rule.

Product-locked scarcity beats general sealed. Classic Collection pack, Pokémon Center promos, Ultra-Premium boxes: the thing you can't get later is the thing that holds.

Boxes over ETBs for the 3–5 year hold. ETBs move faster and plateau sooner.

Underwrite the net, not the sticker. A $150 box sold at $200 nets about $158 after 15% fees and shipping. A 33% move becomes 5%. A buy needs a credible path to at least 2×.

Big-box "retail" is not always MSRP. Target shelf-priced the 30th ETB at $69.99 and Amazon at $89.99 against a $49.99 list; GameStop runs 2–3×. Pokémon Center, Best Buy and your local shop are the true-MSRP channels.
